## 1. Purpose

Owns what reaches a member's attention and when: the notification centre, the families notifications are grouped into, and the member's preferences over them. It answers "what should this member be told, and do they want to hear it".

---

## 2. Glossary

Terms specific to this domain. Where a term means something different here than in
`global_spec.md`, that difference must be stated explicitly.

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Notification** | A message generated by a domain event and addressed to one member |
| **Family** | The grouping a notification belongs to: due dates, payments, returns, reservations, or support |
| **Notification centre** | The header surface listing notifications, with an unread count |
| **Mute** | A member preference suppressing a family, or all families |

---

## 3. Business Rules

Numbered `BR-NTF-{NNN}`. Each rule must be a complete, unambiguous, independently testable
statement. Use "must", never "should". A rule that does not fit in one sentence is probably two rules.
**An ID never changes**, even when the rule text does.

| ID | Rule |
|---|---|
| `BR-NTF-001` | *To be authored.* |

Rules this domain is expected to define:

- The families and which domain events feed each one
- Read, unread, and cleared behaviour
- Global mute and per-family mute, and when a change takes effect
- That selecting a notification navigates to the screen it refers to
- What happens to notifications generated while a family is muted

## 6. Out of Scope

Explicitly **not** handled by this domain. This section is as important as what the domain does handle —
ambiguity about boundaries is the most common source of domain conflicts.

- Email and push delivery channels - the MVP surface is in-application only
- Digest and scheduled summary messages
- Notifications addressed to staff rather than members
